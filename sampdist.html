Sampling Distributions Lab

What is happening?

The model is taking samples from a randomized dataset with the specified statistics. It will then keep taking them, adding their sample means to the model below until it reaches however many are listed as the Total Samples (N).

Why do this?

Since, in statistics, we work with samples more than populations, it's a good idea to understand what sampling distributions wind up looking like.
